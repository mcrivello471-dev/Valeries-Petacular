About Us

At Valerie’s Petacular Doggy Daycare, we provide more than just a place for dogs to stay—we offer a true home environment where every dog is treated like family.

Located in Vernon, CT, our daycare is built around comfort, safety, and personalized attention. Unlike large facilities, we focus on small group sizes to ensure every dog receives the care, supervision, and affection they deserve throughout the day.
Valerie has been around dogs her entire life and brings over 40 years of hands-on experience in dog care, well-being, and boarding.

Her deep understanding of canine behavior, combined with her natural love for animals, creates a calm and nurturing environment that dogs respond to immediately.

She is also certified in Dog CPR and First Aid, providing an added layer of safety and peace of mind for every pet owner.

Our space is designed to feel welcoming and stress-free, giving dogs the freedom to play, socialize, and relax comfortably. Whether it’s outdoor playtime, quiet rest, or simply being around caring people, each dog’s individual needs and personality are always respected.

We understand that leaving your dog with someone else requires trust. That’s why we prioritize safety, structured care, and genuine compassion in everything we do. From the moment your dog arrives, they become part of our extended family.

At Valerie’s Petacular, your dog isn’t just watched—they’re cared for, understood, and loved.

Our Services

At Valerie’s Petacular Doggy Daycare, we offer personalized care designed to meet each dog’s unique needs in a safe, home-style environment. Our services focus on comfort, structure, and giving every dog the attention they deserve.
🐶 Doggy Daycare
A fun and supervised environment where dogs can socialize, play, and relax throughout the day. We maintain small group sizes to ensure safe interactions and individualized attention for every dog.
🚶‍♂️ Walks & Outdoor Play
Regular walks and outdoor time are part of each dog’s day, helping them stay active, happy, and mentally stimulated in a secure setting.
💤 Rest & Relaxation Time
Balanced care is important. Dogs are given time to rest and recharge in a calm, comfortable environment to prevent overstimulation.
⭐ Petacular Plus (Add-On Care)
Extra playtime, additional attention, and personalized care for dogs who thrive with a little more one-on-one interaction.
👑 VIP Day Experience Enhanced care that includes longer walks, priority attention, and a more customized daily routine tailored to your dog’s personality and energy level.
💎 Luxury Package
Our premium experience featuring top-tier care, extended attention, and additional comforts such as grooming or a bath (upon request), plus a personalized report card so you can see how your dog’s day went.
🚗 Pickup & Drop-Off (Optional)
Convenient transportation options may be available to make your schedule easier while ensuring your dog arrives safely and stress-free.
